Plane Radar

See live aircraft flying near your home.
Needs 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi + internet · Powered by USB-C

Setup – 6 steps

1
Plug in. Use the USB cable and any phone charger. The screen lights up.
2
On your phone, open Wi-Fi settings. Join the network PlaneRadar-Setup. No password.
3
A setup page opens. If not, open your web browser and type 192.168.4.1
4
Pick your home Wi-Fi, type the password, tap Save.
5
Wait a few seconds. The radar appears.
6
Set your location (see the box below).
STEP 6 IS IMPORTANT. The radar starts set to Amsterdam, so you must enter your own location.
With your phone on your home Wi-Fi, open plane-radar.local in your browser. Type your Latitude and Longitude, tap Save.
Finding your numbers: in Google Maps, touch and hold on your home. The two numbers shown are your coordinates.
Tip: West and south numbers have a minus sign (-).
Not loading? Use the radar's IP address from your router instead.

The button (marked BOOT)

Quick pressChanges the range (how far the radar sees)
Hold 3 secondsResets Wi-Fi and location. Then start again at step 1

Do not hold the button while plugging in. The screen will stay blank. If this happens, unplug and plug in again without touching the button.

Reading the screen

Aircraft

A triangle points the way it is flying. A line shows its speed. Name, type and height are beside it.

Dot on the edge

A plane too far away. It shows the direction only. Press the button to zoom out.

Rings

Distance circles. The label on the right-hand line gives the distance to the third ring.

Runways

Lines with the airport code, for major airports nearby. Can be turned off on the settings page.

Centre dot = your home

North is always at the top (N, E, S, W marked on the edge).

Planes on the ground

Not shown. Only planes in the air appear.

Care and safety

  • Indoor use. Keep dry, away from heat and strong sun. Handle the glass gently.
  • 5 V USB power only. Unplug before cleaning with a dry cloth.
  • For fun only. Not for navigation or safety use. Data can be late or incomplete.
